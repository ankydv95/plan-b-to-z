# Brand Guidelines: Plan B to Z

## 1. Brand Heart
**Mission:** To transform the narrative of civil services "failure" into a story of powerful career pivots.
**Vision:** To be the default safety net and launchpad for every Indian youth who dares to prepare for the toughest exams in the country.
**Core Values:** Empathy, Practicality, Respect, Forward-Momentum.

## 2. Tone of Voice
Our communication must feel like a conversation with a wise, compassionate senior who truly *gets it*. 

- **Do's:**
  - Use empowering, future-focused language (e.g., "Pivot", "Launchpad", "Next Chapter", "Transition").
  - Validate their hard work (e.g., "The skills you built are extraordinary").
  - Be practical and actionable. Avoid empty motivational fluff; give them real steps.
  - Speak to their intellect. They are smart; treat them as such.

- **Don'ts (Banned Vocabulary):**
  - "Failed" or "Failure" (Use: *Moved on*, *Pivoted*, *Transitioned*).
  - "Dropout" or "Quitter" (Use: *Ex-aspirant*, *Former aspirant*).
  - "Wasted years" (Use: *Years of intense foundation building*).

## 3. Visual Identity
The visual aesthetic should evoke a sense of calm, premium quality, and a new dawn (sunrise). It should feel completely different from chaotic coaching institute websites.

### Color Palette
- **Primary Background (Sand):** `#FEFDFB` - Evokes a blank page, a fresh start.
- **Secondary Background (Warm Beige):** `#FDF6EC`
- **Dark Text/Accents (Dark Espresso):** `#2A1F14` & `#5C4E3D` - For strong, grounding typography.
- **Brand Accents (The "Dawn"):** 
  - Primary Orange: `#D97706` 
  - Vivid Orange: `#EA580C`
- **Functional Colors:**
  - Success/Growth: `#059669` (Green)
  - Intelligence/Tech: `#0284C7` (Blue)
  - Creativity/Wellbeing: `#7C3AED` (Purple)

### Typography
1. **Primary Font (Headings/Quotes):** `Lora` (Serif). 
   - *Why?* It feels intellectual, academic, and deeply human. It respects their reading habits.
2. **Secondary Font (Body/UI):** `Geist` or `Inter` (Sans-serif). 
   - *Why?* High legibility, modern, tech-forward.
3. **Accent Font (Numbers/Code):** `JetBrains Mono` (Monospace).
   - *Why?* Adds a touch of structure and "action-oriented" tech aesthetic.

### Logo & Iconography
- **Logo Treatment:** Text-based. "Plan **B** to Z" with the 'B' stylized (italicized and in brand orange `#D97706`) to emphasize the importance of the backup plan becoming the main plan.
- **Iconography:** Clean, modern, outlined icons (Lucide React). Avoid heavy, cartoonish illustrations.

## 4. UI/UX Principles
- **Clarity over Clutter:** The user is already overwhelmed by life. The UI must be a sanctuary of calm. Use ample whitespace.
- **Micro-interactions:** Soft fade-ins (`animate-fade-up`) and gentle hover states to make the platform feel alive and responsive.
- **Card-based Layouts:** Information should be chunked into easily digestible cards with subtle borders rather than heavy drop shadows.
